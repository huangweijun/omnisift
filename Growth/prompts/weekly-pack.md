# Weekly Growth Pack Prompt

Use the files in `Growth/sources/` as source of truth.

Generate a weekly content pack with:

- one English short blog draft
- one Chinese short blog draft
- one English X thread
- one Chinese X thread
- one English LinkedIn post
- one Chinese LinkedIn post
- one Chinese short video script
- one App Store keyword note

Rules:

- One output = one concrete claim.
- Use plain language.
- Include a concrete capture scenario.
- Mention privacy boundaries accurately.
- Do not claim the AI runs fully locally.
- Do not publish an App Store URL until `docs/site.js` has one.
- Do not use hype words such as revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, all-in-one, or ultimate.
- End with a light CTA: launch notice before approval, App Store download after approval.
