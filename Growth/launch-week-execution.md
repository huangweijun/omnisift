### 即刻 / V2EX / 少数派草稿

我做了一个很小的 iOS 工具，叫知漏 / OmniSift。

它解决的问题不是「再做一个笔记软件」，而是更早一步：当一段有用内容出现在 ChatGPT、Claude、Safari、微信、截图里时，先把它抓住。

使用方式很简单：

1. 从 iOS 分享菜单把文本、链接、文章或截图发给知漏
2. AI 整理成标题、摘要、高亮、标签、实体和关系
3. 生成的知识卡片保存在设备本地

隐私边界也明确：无需账号、无分析追踪；主动分享的文本会发送到云端 AI 做处理，不声称完全本地 AI。

## Day 4: LinkedIn / Work-Focused Post

Most note-taking tools start from a blank page.

That is useful when you already know what you want to write. It is less useful when the idea appears somewhere else first: inside an AI answer, a browser paragraph, a chat message, or a screenshot.

OmniSift is built around that earlier moment. On iOS, you share text, links, articles, or screenshots from another app. OmniSift uses cloud AI to turn the snippet into a structured knowledge card: title, summary, highlights, tags, named entities, and relations.

The boundary matters: no account required, no analytics tracking, cards stored locally on device, and shared text sent to cloud AI for processing only.

Download: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/omnisift/id6773056945?uo=4

### Show HN body

I built OmniSift because I kept losing useful fragments from AI answers, articles, chats, and screenshots before they made it into a real note.

It is an iOS Share Sheet workflow: share text, links, articles, or screenshots, and OmniSift turns the fragment into a structured card with a title, summary, highlights, tags, entities, and relations.

The product boundary is intentionally narrow. It is not a long-form note editor. It is for capture before organizing.

Privacy boundary: no account, no analytics tracking, cards stored locally on device. Shared text is sent to cloud AI for processing only.
