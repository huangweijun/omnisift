# Use Cases

## AI conversation capture

Long ChatGPT or Claude answers often contain a few parts worth saving. OmniSift is for capturing the useful section and turning it into a card with summary, highlights, tags, entities, and relations.

## Web reading

When an article has one paragraph worth revisiting, save the text or link through the Share Sheet. OmniSift keeps the source context and converts the reading moment into a structured card.

## Research fragments

Research rarely arrives in one clean document. It appears as snippets from papers, web pages, conversations, screenshots, and saved links. OmniSift helps capture those fragments first, then connect repeated concepts later.

## Study cards

Students can save definitions, explanations, formulas, and examples from different apps. AI-generated summaries and tags make the card easier to review.

## Screenshot OCR

Some apps do not expose clean copying. Screenshots give users a fallback capture path for chats, documents, feeds, and app screens.

## Idea inbox

Small ideas often disappear because opening a full note app feels too heavy. OmniSift is useful when capture needs to be faster than organization.

## Best fit

Use OmniSift when:

- the idea appears outside the notes app
- the input is short or medium length
- structure is missing
- source context matters
- saving must be quick

Use a note app when:

- the user wants to write a long original note
- the user is drafting or editing a full document
- the user needs team collaboration or databases
