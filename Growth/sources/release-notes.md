## v1.0 focus

- Share Extension capture through the iOS Share Sheet
- Clipboard-assisted capture for copied images and copied Xiaohongshu links with confirmation
- AI-powered knowledge card generation
- Readable Markdown originals when source text can be parsed
- Local card storage
- Personal knowledge star map and topic constellations
- Knowledge compass for lit themes, dim areas, patterns, and next routes
- Related insights discovery
- Free tier with 5 AI processing uses per day
- Pro tier designed for 50 AI processing uses per day
- Pro first-release pricing planned at ¥12/month or ¥68/year when available through App Store subscriptions
