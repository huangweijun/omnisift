# Distribution Source

## First channels

- Website: https://omnisift.app/
- App Store product page after approval
- X / Threads posts
- LinkedIn post
- Indie Hackers launch note
- Product Hunt draft after App Store URL exists
- Show HN draft after App Store URL exists
- Chinese platforms: 即刻, 少数派, 小红书 draft, V2EX when appropriate

## Manual submission targets

- Google Search Console: submit `https://omnisift.app/sitemap.xml`
- Bing Webmaster Tools: submit `https://omnisift.app/sitemap.xml`
- Product Hunt: prepare page only after App Store URL exists
- AlternativeTo: submit only after public product URL and screenshots are ready

## Publishing guardrails

- No fake reviews.
- No bulk spam.
- No auto-posting before human review.
- No "fully local AI" claim.
- No unconfirmed App Store link.
- One post should carry one concrete claim.
