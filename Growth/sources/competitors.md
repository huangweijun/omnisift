# Competitor Notes

This file is for gentle positioning, not attack copy.

## Apple Notes

Apple Notes is good for writing notes, quick lists, sketches, and long-term storage inside the Apple ecosystem.

OmniSift differs by focusing on fast capture from other apps and automatic card structure: summary, highlights, tags, entities, and relations.

## Notion

Notion is good for databases, team workspaces, project docs, and highly customized pages.

OmniSift differs by avoiding account-first workspace setup and focusing on mobile Share Sheet capture with local card storage.

## Readwise

Readwise is good for reader highlights, review workflows, and resurfacing saved reading highlights.

OmniSift differs by accepting broader iOS inputs, including AI answers, plain text snippets, links, articles, and screenshots.

## Pocket

Pocket is good for saving articles and links to read later.

OmniSift differs by turning a captured fragment into a structured knowledge card instead of only keeping a reading queue.

## Safe comparison language

- "Different tools fit different parts of the workflow."
- "OmniSift focuses on one narrow job."
- "Use OmniSift when the input is scattered and the structure is missing."
- "Use your note app when you already know you want to write a full page."

Avoid:

- claiming other tools are obsolete
- claiming OmniSift replaces all note-taking
- unverified feature comparisons
- aggressive "vs" copy
