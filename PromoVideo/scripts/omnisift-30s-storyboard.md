# OmniSift 30s Promo Storyboard

Format: vertical 1080x1920, 30fps, 30 seconds.
Tone: real product walkthrough, not an abstract concept trailer.
Visual rule: show app-like screens with OmniSift demo data from `ScreenshotDemoData.swift`.

## 0.0-3.5s - Problem In Context

Visual:
- A Safari-like article and a ChatGPT-like answer sit behind an iPhone.
- A useful paragraph is highlighted.
- System share sheet appears, OmniSift is selected.

On-screen copy:
- "Ideas appear everywhere."
- "Save the useful part before it disappears."

Product point:
- OmniSift starts from cross-app capture, not blank-page note writing.

## 3.5-7.5s - Share Extension Capture

Visual:
- Realistic OmniSift share extension bottom panel.
- Incoming signal preview shows source title, link, and selected text.
- User taps "Save to OmniSift".

On-screen copy:
- "Share text, links, articles, or screenshots."
- "No manual filing first."

Product point:
- Capture is quick and explicit.

## 7.5-11.5s - AI Processing

Visual:
- Processing card in the Library.
- The card fills in title, highlight, summary, tags, entities, and relations.

On-screen copy:
- "AI turns fragments into structured cards."
- "Title, highlight, summary, tags, entities, relations."

Product point:
- The value appears after capture.

## 11.5-16.5s - Library And Search

Visual:
- Card list with segmented Inbox/Library control, topic chips, source badges.
- Demo cards:
  - AI reading workflow
  - SwiftData migration checklist
  - Screenshot OCR idea capture
  - Pricing and usage limits
- Search narrows to "AI Research".

On-screen copy:
- "Browse by topic, source, and search."

Product point:
- OmniSift becomes a searchable knowledge library.

## 16.5-21.0s - Card Detail

Visual:
- Detail screen for "AI reading workflow".
- Summary tab, highlight quote, summary, topics/tags/entities, related insights.
- Quick flash of Original and Source tabs.

On-screen copy:
- "Every save becomes reviewable."
- "Related insights surface automatically."

Product point:
- Cards are useful to revisit, not just stored.

## 21.0-26.0s - Knowledge Map

Visual:
- Knowledge Map screen with Knowledge Galaxy panel.
- Metrics: 4 constellations, 4 stars, 9 entities, 6 links.
- Topic constellations light up and connect.

On-screen copy:
- "Cards light your personal knowledge star map."

Product point:
- Repeated concepts become visible.

## 26.0-29.0s - Knowledge Compass

Visual:
- Compass card rotates through Lit, Pattern, Dark, Route.
- Route card recommends what to explore next.

On-screen copy:
- "The compass points to what to explore next."

Product point:
- The app guides next-step learning.

## 29.0-30.0s - Closing

Visual:
- App icon, OmniSift / 知漏, App Store CTA.

On-screen copy:
- "Capture ideas. Light your map."
- "OmniSift"
