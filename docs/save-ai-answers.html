AI answer capture

Save AI answers before they disappear.

ChatGPT and Claude often produce one useful paragraph inside a long answer. OmniSift helps capture that fragment from iOS and turn it into a structured card you can review later.

1

Select or share the useful part

When an answer contains a section worth keeping, share the text, link, or screenshot to OmniSift from the iOS Share Sheet.

2

Let AI structure the fragment

OmniSift uses cloud AI to extract a title, summary, highlights, tags, named entities, and semantic relations.

3

Keep the card locally

The processed knowledge card is stored locally on your device. OmniSift requires no account and uses no analytics tracking.

4

Review connected ideas later

Related cards, topic constellations, the knowledge star map, and compass guidance make saved fragments easier to revisit.

When to use it

Use OmniSift when copying into a note app feels too heavy.

If you already want to write a full note, use your note app. If a fragment might matter later but you do not know where it belongs yet, capture it first.

  • ✓Save useful ChatGPT or Claude answers from iPhone.
  • ✓Capture browser paragraphs, chat messages, screenshots, and copied images.
  • ✓Turn scattered snippets into cards with summaries, tags, entities, and relations.
  • ✓Keep processed cards local while sending shared text to cloud AI only for processing.
