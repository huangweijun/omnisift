Capture first, organize later

Fast collection keeps useful ideas from disappearing. Structure can be created after the moment is safely saved.

Capture Share Sheet Knowledge Card

Do this in Safari

Three taps to make the first card.

1

Tap Safari Share

Use the system Share button at the bottom of Safari. If you opened this page from OmniSift, you are already in the right place.

点击 Safari 底部的系统分享按钮。如果你是从知漏打开这页，已经在正确的位置。

2

Choose OmniSift

If OmniSift is not visible, tap More, enable OmniSift, and move it near the top for faster future captures.

如果没有看到知漏，点击“更多”，启用知漏，并把它移到靠前位置，之后分享会更快。

3

Save and return

OmniSift saves the page first, then organizes it into a structured card with a title, summary, tags, and source link.

知漏会先保存这页，再整理成包含标题、摘要、标签和来源链接的结构化卡片。

Sample insight

Capture first, organize later.

The best knowledge workflow on a phone starts with a small promise: save the useful thing before deciding where it belongs. When capture is fast, ideas do not disappear while you are choosing folders, tags, or a perfect title.

手机上的知识工作流，最好从一个小承诺开始：先保存有用的东西，再决定它该放在哪里。当捕获足够快，想法就不会在你纠结文件夹、标签或完美标题时消失。

OmniSift turns that captured fragment into a card. A card can keep the source link, summarize the point, extract topics, and connect repeated people, tools, concepts, or questions across your library.

知漏会把捕获到的片段变成卡片。卡片可以保留来源链接，总结重点，提取主题，并把反复出现的人物、工具、概念或问题连接起来。

This page is intentionally short and clean. It is a safe first capture because Safari can share it consistently, and the resulting card should be easy to recognize in your OmniSift library.

这页故意写得短而干净。它适合作为第一次捕获，因为 Safari 的分享链路更稳定，生成的卡片也更容易在知漏知识库里识别。
