Use cases

For the moments when an idea appears outside your notes app.

OmniSift is designed for knowledge workers, students, researchers, founders, and curious readers who collect useful fragments across many iOS apps.

AI

Save ChatGPT and Claude answers

Long AI answers often contain one or two parts you want to keep. Share the useful section to OmniSift and turn it into a card with summary, highlights, tags, and entities.

长 AI 回答里经常只有一两段真正值得保留。把有用部分分享到知漏，自动生成摘要、高亮、标签和实体。

Web

Capture web reading

When an article has a paragraph worth revisiting, save the link or text. OmniSift keeps the source attached and converts the reading moment into a structured card.

读网页时遇到值得回看的段落，可以保存链接或正文。知漏会保留来源，并把阅读瞬间变成结构化卡片。

OCR

Turn screenshots into knowledge

Screenshot text from chats, documents, feeds, or apps that do not expose clean copying. OmniSift can capture image text and organize it with your other cards.

对聊天、文档、信息流或不方便复制的 App 截图，知漏可以识别图片文字，并与其他卡片一起整理。

Study

Build study cards from highlights

Collect definitions, examples, quotes, and references while studying. Tags and summaries make later review faster than digging through source apps.

学习时收集定义、例子、引用和参考资料。标签和摘要让复习比回到原 App 翻找更快。

Research

Connect research fragments

OmniSift extracts entities and relations so repeated people, concepts, tools, and sources can start forming a personal knowledge graph.

知漏会提取实体和关系，让反复出现的人物、概念、工具和来源逐渐形成个人知识图谱。

Ideas

Keep small ideas from disappearing

Save the useful thing when you see it, even if you do not know where it belongs yet. Structure can happen after capture.

先保存看见的有用内容，即使暂时不知道该放在哪里。结构可以在捕获之后再生成。

Workflow fit

Best when capture needs to be faster than organization.

If you already know you want to write a full page, use your note app. If you found a fragment that might matter later, send it to OmniSift.
